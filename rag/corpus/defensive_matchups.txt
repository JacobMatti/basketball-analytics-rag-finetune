Defensive Matchup Analysis

Matchup data identifies which defender is primarily responsible for guarding a given
offensive player on each possession, usually inferred from tracking data by finding the
defender who stays closest to an offensive player over the course of a possession
weighted by proximity when the ball is near that player. Once matchups are assigned,
analysts can build matchup-adjusted efficiency metrics, such as how a player performs
in isolation against specific defender archetypes, or how a defense's efficiency changes
when a primary rim protector is switched onto a smaller, quicker ball handler. Switch-
heavy defensive schemes create a large number of unusual matchups within a single
game, which makes matchup-level data especially valuable for evaluating how a defense
holds up against different play types, and for identifying which specific defenders create
the most disruption when switched onto ball handlers versus screeners.
