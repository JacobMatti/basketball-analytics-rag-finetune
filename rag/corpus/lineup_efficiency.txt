Lineup Efficiency and Net Rating

Lineup-level efficiency metrics measure points scored and allowed per 100 possessions
for every five-player combination that shares the floor together. Net rating for a lineup is
simply offensive rating minus defensive rating over the minutes that specific group has
played. Because most five-man combinations only play a small number of possessions
together in a season, raw lineup net ratings are noisy and are usually regularized toward
a prior based on the individual players' broader on-off impact. Analysts also build
adjusted plus-minus style models that isolate an individual player's marginal impact on
scoring margin, controlling for the quality of teammates and opponents on the floor at
the same time. These regularized, player-level estimates are generally more stable and
more useful for roster construction decisions than raw lineup net rating alone, especially
early in a season when sample sizes are still small.
