Pick-and-Roll Defense Coverages

Teams defend the pick-and-roll using a handful of common coverage schemes, each with
different tracking-data signatures. In drop coverage, the screener's defender retreats
toward the basket to protect the rim while the ball handler's defender fights over the
screen, which tends to concede more mid-range shots but limits shots at the rim. In
blitz or hard hedge coverage, both defenders converge on the ball handler immediately
after the screen, which pressures the ball but temporarily leaves the screener's
defender out of position, creating short-roll opportunities if the ball handler can pass
out of the trap. Switching coverage simply exchanges defensive assignments at the
point of the screen, avoiding a temporary numbers disadvantage but often creating a
size or speed mismatch elsewhere on the floor. Coverage choice by team and by
individual matchup is one of the most common questions strategic staffs bring to
analytics groups before a playoff series.
